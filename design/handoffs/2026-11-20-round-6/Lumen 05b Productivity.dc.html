TCP handshake

Before any data flows, client and server agree on initial sequence numbers in three steps: SYN, SYN-ACK, ACK.

Each side picks a random ISN so that delayed segments from an old connection can't be mistaken for new ones. After the final ACK both ends are ESTABLISHED.
