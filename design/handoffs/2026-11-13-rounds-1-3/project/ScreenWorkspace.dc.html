Gradient descent

Minimize a loss J(θ) by stepping against its gradient.

θt+1 = θt − η ∇J(θt)
Choosing ηToo large diverges, too small crawls. Start at 1e-3, then sweep.
Variants
Batch GD Stochastic GD Mini-batch + momentum
