Gradient descent

Updated 3 d ago·6 min read·#optimization#ml

Gradient descent minimizes a loss J(θ) by stepping against its gradient. It is the workhorse behind almost everything in Backpropagation, and its step size is bounded by the largest eigenvalue — see Linear algebra: eigenvectors.

The update rule

θt+1 = θt − η ∇J(θt)
Tip — start at 1e-3, then sweep. My notes from Meeting with Dr. Lim have the exact grid.

Momentum adds a velocity term so the steps stop zig-zagging across narrow valleys…
